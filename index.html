How it works

From job post to hired in four steps

Kashif handles the repetitive work so you focus on the conversations that matter.

  1. Step 01

    Post the role in minutes

    Create a job with title, description, and optional screening questions. Hit Publish and Kashif generates a shareable careers page link — copy it to LinkedIn, your website, or anywhere candidates are.

  2. Step 02

    Applicants apply — Kashif screens automatically

    Candidates submit a resume or their LinkedIn URL. Within 2 minutes, Kashif parses their profile, enriches it if needed, and assigns an AI fit score with a plain-English explanation. No recruiter action required.

  3. Step 03

    Review your ranked shortlist

    Open the pipeline to find applicants already sorted by fit score. Review the AI reasoning, advance promising candidates, and bulk-reject the rest with a polite template email — all from one screen.

  4. Step 04

    Book interviews and make the offer

    Generate a self-scheduling link, send it with one click, and your calendar is booked. Track offer and hire stages in the pipeline so the whole team stays in sync.
