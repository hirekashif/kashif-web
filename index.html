Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before switching from spreadsheets and email threads.

How does the public careers page work?
When you publish a job in Kashif, a unique public URL is generated (e.g. hirekashif.com/careers/your-role-abc1). That page is mobile-friendly, branded with your company name, and includes your job description, any custom screening questions, and a resume upload form. Share the link anywhere — no account or login is required for applicants.
What happens when a candidate applies?
Kashif immediately parses the uploaded resume (or enriches via LinkedIn if no resume is provided), extracts skills and experience, and runs an AI fit score against your job's criteria. Within about 2 minutes, the candidate appears in your shortlist ranked by score with an evidence summary — strengths, gaps, and a plain-English explanation. Zero manual steps required.
Can I also search for candidates proactively?
Yes — the Discovery tab lets you search for candidates by title, skills, and location. When you add a sourced candidate to your pipeline, Kashif automatically enriches their LinkedIn profile and runs the same AI scoring. Outbound and inbound candidates live in the same ranked shortlist so you compare them on equal footing.
Can I show the jobs on my own website?
Yes — two ways. Every workspace gets a clean careers page on its own hirekashif.com subdomain (e.g. yourcompany.hirekashif.com). And on Pro, you can drop your live open roles straight onto your existing site with a one-line embed snippet — it stays in sync automatically as you publish or close roles, and matches your brand. Candidates still apply through Kashif, so screening and compliance are handled for you.
How is candidate data kept secure between organisations?
Every request passes through an auth middleware layer that binds a unique organisation identifier to the session. Every database query is scoped to that org — it is architecturally impossible to read another workspace's data. Tenant isolation is enforced at the query level, not just the UI, and is covered by an automated integration test suite run on every deployment.
Can I customise what the AI scores against?
Yes. Each job has a calibration section where you define required vs. nice-to-have skills, experience levels, and seniority expectations. The AI reads these as scoring rules, so the fit percentages reflect your specific criteria — not a generic hiring algorithm.
