# Interviews & Scheduling

Kashif books interviews on your **own** connected calendar (Google or Microsoft) and lets
candidates self-schedule — no back-and-forth email threads.

> **Prerequisite:** connect Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 with calendar sync enabled. See
> [Integrations](integrations.md). Without a connected calendar, scheduling still works as a
> recorded plan, but no live calendar event is created.

## Candidate self-scheduling (recommended)

From a candidate's review drawer:

1. Propose times. Either add slots manually, or click **Suggest open slots** to have Kashif
   propose genuinely free working-hour times from your connected calendar (see below).
2. Click **Generate self-booking link**. Kashif creates a tokenized link (valid 7 days).
3. Share it — copy it, or send it to the candidate through your connected mailbox.

The candidate opens the link (no login), picks one of the offered times, and confirms. Kashif then:

- books a **real calendar event** on your primary calendar (with the candidate as attendee),
- advances the pipeline entry to **interview**,
- records the booking and logs it to contact history.

## Suggest open slots (free/busy)

**Suggest open slots** reads your connected calendar's free/busy for the next several business days
and proposes open times within working hours, skipping anything you're already busy with. If no
calendar is connected, it falls back to plain working-hour slots (which may collide with real
events — connect a calendar to avoid that).

## Direct booking

If you'd rather book directly (you already agreed a time), use the direct-book option in the
schedule form. Kashif creates the calendar event immediately and moves the candidate to interview.

## Reminders & rescheduling

- **Reminders** — Kashif sends the candidate a reminder about **24 hours before** the interview,
  through your mailbox, including a reschedule link and their status link.
- **Rescheduling** — if the candidate rebooks via a reschedule link, Kashif **cancels the previous
  calendar event** and books the new one.

## Interview feedback

Capture structured **scorecards** (competency ratings + recommendation) per interviewer from the
candidate's review drawer, and generate an **AI interview guide** (focus areas and questions
tailored to the candidate and role). The **scorecard consensus** view rolls feedback up into a
recommendation. See [Applicants & pipeline](applicants-and-pipeline.md).
