# Integrations

Kashif connects to your **own** Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 so candidate email and interview
invites come from your company — not a platform relay — and to Slack for internal alerts.

## Why connect a mailbox

Connecting a workspace mailbox unlocks the "never ghost a candidate" experience:

- **Application confirmations**, **decision emails**, and **interview reminders** are sent from
  your mailbox (great deliverability, replies land with you).
- **Interview invites** create real calendar events.
- **Free/busy slot suggestions** read your calendar to propose conflict-free times.

Without a connected mailbox, candidate emails are skipped and your dashboard shows a **"Connect a
mailbox so applicants hear back"** reminder. Candidate mail is never sent through a shared platform
address.

## Google Workspace

1. Go to **Integrations → Google Workspace** and click **Connect**.
2. Sign in and grant access to send mail and manage calendar events.
3. Choose whether to enable **calendar sync** (needed for booking and free/busy).

Once connected, Gmail send and Google Calendar are available across the app.

## Microsoft 365

1. Go to **Integrations → Microsoft 365** and click **Connect**.
2. Sign in with your Microsoft work account and grant mail + calendar access.
3. Enable **calendar sync** if you want booking and free/busy.

Outlook mail and Microsoft Calendar then work identically to the Google path.

## Choosing your primary provider

If both are connected, set a **primary email provider** so Kashif knows which mailbox to send
candidate mail from. Calendar booking and free/busy follow the same resolution.

## Slack

Connect **Slack** (incoming webhook) to get internal notifications — new candidates, stage moves,
and scorecards — in a channel your team watches. Slack is for **internal** alerts only; candidate
communication always goes through your email provider.

## Branding your candidate pages

On paid plans, set your **logo, primary color, and tagline** under **Settings → Branding**. These
theme your careers pages, the candidate status page, and scheduling pages so the whole candidate
experience looks like your company.

## Other connectors

**ATS** and **job board** connectors are provider-neutral placeholders today — the workspace can
store configuration, but active sync/publishing isn't live yet. Check back as these mature.
