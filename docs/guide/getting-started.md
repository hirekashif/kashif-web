# Getting Started

This guide takes a brand-new user from sign-up to a published job with applicants flowing in.

## 1. Create your workspace

You can sign up two ways:

- **Email + password** — enter your organization name, your name, email, and a password.
- **Continue with Google** — one click; your workspace is created on first sign-in.

Either way:

- A **workspace** (your company's tenant) is created and you become its **Owner (admin)**.
- You start a **14-day full-feature Pro trial** automatically — no card required.

## 2. Verify your email

Password sign-ups receive a verification email. You can keep using Kashif right away, but a few
actions require a verified address:

- **Inviting teammates**
- **Connecting integrations** *(may be enabled depending on your deployment)*

Click the link in the email (it lands on a confirmation page). If it expired, request a new one
from **Settings**. Google sign-ups are verified automatically.

## 3. (Recommended) Turn on two-factor authentication

Under **Settings → Security**, enable MFA. You'll scan a QR code with an authenticator app and
save the backup codes. Once enabled, both password and Google sign-in ask for the 6-digit code on
a dedicated verification step. See [Team, roles & security](team-roles-and-security.md).

## 4. Post your first job

From the dashboard, the primary action for a new workspace is **Create your first job**.

- Fill in the title (and optionally seniority, location, employment type), or use
  **✨ Draft with AI** to generate a description, suggested skills, and apply questions from just
  the title.
- Optionally add **apply questions** and **knockout rules** (e.g. auto-decline if not authorized
  to work). See [Jobs & careers pages](jobs-and-careers.md).

## 5. Publish and share

Click **Publish role**. Kashif mints a public careers link. Use:

- **Copy link** — the per-job apply page.
- **Copy careers page** — your company page listing all open roles.

Share either link. Applicants apply with a resume or LinkedIn URL — no account needed.

## 6. Watch the shortlist build itself

As applications arrive, Kashif automatically:

1. Parses the resume into structured fields.
2. Runs an **AI fit score** with cited evidence against your role calibration.
3. Ranks applicants so the strongest rise to the top of the pipeline.
4. Sends each applicant a **confirmation email** with a private status link.

Your dashboard's **action queue** shows "N new applicants to review." Open the job to review the
ranked pipeline. From there you shortlist, schedule interviews, and make decisions — with every
candidate kept informed. Continue with [Applicants & pipeline](applicants-and-pipeline.md).

## First-session checklist

- [ ] Workspace created and email verified
- [ ] MFA enabled (recommended)
- [ ] A mailbox connected (Google/Microsoft) so candidates get emails — see [Integrations](integrations.md)
- [ ] First job created and published
- [ ] Careers link shared
